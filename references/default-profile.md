# Default Briefing Profile

Use these defaults only when the user does not specify otherwise.

## Audience

A Chinese-speaking generalist who wants high-signal updates and can understand mainstream technology and finance terminology, but does not want a specialist research memo.

## Topic mix

Treat these as editorial priors, not fixed quotas:

- AI: about 30%
  - frontier models and major product launches
  - AI companies and platform strategy
  - research with credible practical significance
  - AI policy, safety, chips, infrastructure
- Technology: about 25%
  - major platforms and devices
  - cybersecurity
  - semiconductors/cloud/developer ecosystem
  - consequential regulation or antitrust
- Finance / markets: about 25%
  - macro data and central banks
  - market-moving earnings/deals
  - major equity, rates, FX, commodity, or crypto developments when genuinely material
- World affairs: about 20%
  - geopolitics, conflict, elections/governance, trade, major disasters, public-health events, and policy changes with broad consequences

## Geography

Maintain a global view. Give extra weight to developments involving China, the United States, Europe, and major Asian economies when their impact is broad, but do not force geographic balance at the expense of importance.

## Reading length

Select 6-10 stories. Target 80-120 Chinese characters of body text per story, plus a bold headline and one original-source link. Keep the normal edition around a five-minute read and shorten on unusually quiet news days.

## Tone

Use neutral, analytical, non-sensational Chinese. Lead with what happened and the key facts. Do not append a separate “为什么重要”, “影响”, or “意义” field unless the user explicitly asks for analysis.
